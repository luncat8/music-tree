# 0.1.0 — The lineage explorer

## A playable field guide

The atlas follows ten connected genres from the blues through jazz, rhythm & blues and rock. An edge represents one selected influence, not an exclusive parent or a claim that all music descends from one origin. Dates describe emergence, not a single founding event. A genre file explains what distinguishes it from its parent, its instruments and techniques, a named recording and a practical study piece.

## Interaction and ownership

- The explorer pairs a three-generation map with the selected genre file. Color, labels and connection ports distinguish families. Jazz is the opening selection. Branch filtering, panning, anchored zoom and fit operate on the map, not the page.
- Canvas draws connections. Native buttons on the same transformed coordinate plane provide labeled, keyboard-operable nodes. Resizing lays out the visible leaves and centers each ancestor over its descendants.
- The profile has Overview, Sound and Listening pages. Its player synthesizes a brief, original rhythm/harmony study on demand. Switching genre or leaving the explorer stops playback. The photographs illustrate families, not particular historical recordings.
- A searchable quick jump and an alphabetical genre index open the same files. The guide explains scope and navigation. Narrow screens stack the map and profile.
- The catalog owns the tree and editorial records; the player owns the scores and audio context. The composer wires features together. Scripts, images and typefaces load locally without a build step or a server.

## Acceptance and manual pass

1. Open `index.html` directly; verify the complete tree, legible ports and Jazz file, including its plate and typefaces.
2. Select an offshoot, trace its parent, filter a branch, drag, zoom and fit. Confirm the profile and selected node agree.
3. Search by keyboard, open an index entry and return to the map. Try an empty search, Escape and a pointer-only path.
4. Compare a swung pattern with a straight one; check stop, finish and selection changes. Both listening recommendations name pieces.
5. Check narrow and wide layouts, visible keyboard focus, readable node labels and dialog dismissal. No internet connection is necessary.
