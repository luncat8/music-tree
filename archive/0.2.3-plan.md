# 0.2.3 — The UK rave route

## Selected lineages

The R&B branch follows House → Breakbeat hardcore → Jungle → Drum and bass. The line through house represents the UK acid-house rave route, not a single origin for hardcore. Dashed arrows credit Techno and Hip-hop as additional influences on hardcore and Funk as a source of jungle breaks. The jungle profile credits Jamaican sound-system culture for the low end and MC tradition. The drum and bass profile explains that the two labels overlap.

The twenty-genre atlas adds a landmark recording and a piece to learn to each new file. Breakbeat hardcore pairs SL2’s “On a Ragga Tip” with 2 Bad Mice’s “Bombscare”; jungle pairs “Original Nuttah” with Origin Unknown’s “Valley of the Shadows.” The index and quick search distinguish jungle from drum and bass. Existing file numbers stay stable.

Hardcore’s original 148 BPM study layers keyboard stabs and a regular kick under a syncopated snare pattern. The 160 BPM jungle study breaks up the kick and snare against a slower bass phrase. Drum and bass keeps its 172 BPM study, with a steadier snare backbeat for comparison. Playback requires a user gesture. Both typefaces are bundled locally with their original licenses.

Editorial references: the Rage club lineage [5](https://mixmag.net/feature/25-parties-that-changed-dance-music-forever), an early jungle pressing [1](https://www.londonmuseum.org.uk/collections/v/object-1044893/we-are-ie/), and hardcore repertoire [1](https://www.theguardian.com/music/2020/aug/27/the-greatest-hardcore-rave-tracks-ranked).

## Map and navigation

Column headings mark lineage depth, not a timeline. The map reserves enough width per column for legible names at 100%, even if the stage is narrower. Pan to later columns; Fit shows all eight columns. A drag can start on a card without opening its profile. Selecting a profile or reaching a node with the keyboard brings its card into view. Returning to the map or jumping from search shows the selected card at a readable scale. Branch filters change the visible rows without shifting the column positions.

## Acceptance and manual pass

1. Open `index.html` over `file://` at 320 px, at a short desktop viewport, and at a wide desktop viewport. At 100%, pan across the map and check that the cards and headings do not overlap. Use Fit and confirm every column and both typefaces render without network requests.
2. Open the R&B branch and follow House → Breakbeat hardcore → Jungle → Drum and bass via “Led to”, then follow the links back to each primary parent. Open “jungle” and “drum n bass” from quick search. On mobile, return to the map; on the keyboard, Tab across a viewport edge.
3. Check both listening pieces and their optional references in the new files. Play the three studies and compare the bass pulse and drum accents. Changing genres or leaving the explorer stops playback.
