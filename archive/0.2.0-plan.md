# 0.2.0 — Cross-influences and reference links

## Lineages beyond a single parent

The atlas retains one primary parent per genre to anchor a clear three-generation tree while recording separate cross-influences where one style directly shaped another — including R&B into rock, swing into bebop, soul and jazz into funk, and blues into heavy metal. Genre profiles replace decorative family plates with a typographic header and link genres, instruments, artists and recordings to Wikipedia when an unambiguous article exists.

## Interaction and ownership

- The map draws solid family-colored cables for primary branches and directional dashed arcs in the source family’s color for additional influences. Resting influence arcs stay quiet beneath primary cables; selecting either endpoint raises the arc and its arrowhead. The map key distinguishes branch families from dashed influence links, and branch filtering shows only arcs whose endpoints are both visible.
- The profile header presents family, title, emergence date, origin and a genre Wikipedia link without portrait artwork. Overview pairs the shift narrative and clickable parent link with "Also shaped by" influence notes, core instruments and "Led to" offshoot and influence buttons so readers can traverse the graph from either panel.
- Instrument chips in Overview and Sound open their Wikipedia articles in a new tab when mapped in the catalog and render as static chips otherwise. Listening uses structured piece records (`title`, optional `wiki`, `artist`, optional `ensemble`, optional `artistWiki`, `year`, `note`), linking songs and artists independently when available.
- The catalog owns influence pairs, instrument slugs, structured recordings and Wikipedia URL formation, and indexes artists, ensembles and pieces for search. All runtime scripts, icons and licensed typefaces remain local with zero automatic network requests; deferred genre plates and low-priority instrument illustrations live in `plan-deferred.md`.

## Acceptance and manual pass

1. Open `index.html` directly over `file://`; confirm local Space Grotesk and Plex Mono load cleanly, zero external requests fire on boot, and Jazz opens with a typographic header and Wikipedia badge.
2. Select Rock, R&B, Funk, Bebop and Heavy metal; confirm dashed influence arcs and arrowheads brighten on selection, quiet at rest, and never collide with labels across narrow and wide viewports.
3. Follow a parent kicker link, an "Also shaped by" button and a "Led to" chip; verify a cross-branch jump from a filtered map restores all branches and keeps keyboard focus inside the profile.
4. Check Wikipedia links on a genre header, core and additional instruments, an artist credit and both listening pieces (`target="_blank"`, `rel="noopener noreferrer"`), and confirm unmapped items (`Second guitar`, `Ko-Ko`) render cleanly without broken anchors.
5. Verify `/` search by artist and song, index navigation, guide copy and clean pattern playback start and fade-out stop.
