# Deferred and low-priority plans

## Deferred — Genre and family plates

Illustrative and photographic plates on genre profiles are deferred. Each genre file relies on a typographic header, family tone, lineage links, synthesized pattern studies and external reference links. Scene portraits struggle to represent broad traditions without cliché and take vertical space away from editorial notes and navigation.

## Low priority — Instrument imagery

Reference illustrations or photographs of individual instruments remain an option at low priority, behind lineage expansion, repertoire depth and pattern playback.

- Scope artwork to individual instrument entries or optional disclosures, never genre headers.
- Bundle compact, local files with permissive licenses stored beside the assets so direct `file://` use stays fast and self-contained.
- Keep framing, scale and dark-surface contrast uniform across instrument families.
