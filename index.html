Reading the tree.

This volume follows fourteen genres from the blues into jazz, R&B and rock, then on to disco, hip-hop, jazz fusion and alternative rock. Solid lines trace each genre’s primary branch; dashed lines mark additional influences, including ones that cross between families.

01 / Explore
Select a node to read its file and follow parent or influence links. Filter by branch, drag the canvas to pan, or scroll over it to zoom. Fit restores the whole map.
02 / Listen
Play a short, synthesized study of the selected style. Sound covers the pattern and instruments; Listening pairs a landmark recording with a piece to learn.
03 / Find
Press / to search by name, instrument, artist or place. Use the arrow keys and Enter to open a result, or browse the genre index.

Dates are approximate. Patterns are original studies, not excerpts from the recordings. Reference links open Wikipedia in a new tab when online.
